Brand incubation

Many new brands come to us asking for popular formulas — but that puts things in the wrong order.

The same face cream requires different claims, packaging, and price positioning in Southeast Asia versus Europe. We work backward from the target market: category positioning and price band first, then SKU structure and launch timeline, and only then do we discuss formulation. This reverse approach produces product lines that are viable in the real market.

What We Don't Take On

We don't take one-off orders.

Every order starts with production capacity matching and regulatory pre-assessment. We're not being selective — orders that can't clear these two hurdles won't pass market filing downstream. Better to be upfront than to take the order and tell you it's stuck in registration later.

Starting a new beauty brand from zero: how far can the contract manufacturer help?

Help starts at category positioning. You don't need a formula first. What drains most new brands isn't production — it's juggling six suppliers at once: skincare from Supplier A, color cosmetics from B, oral care from C, each with its own lead times, packaging, and documentation. All six categories sit under one system, with 300+ pre-negotiated strategic suppliers. One order covers multiple categories. 3–7 day rapid sampling. 13,000+ formulas on file. New categories don't require a fresh R&D cycle.

I only have an idea, no formula and no packaging — can we still talk?
Yes. The sequence is: lock category positioning and price band first, then define the SKU structure and launch rhythm, and only then discuss formulas and packaging. Many new brands lead with "what's your hit formula?" — that's the wrong order. The same face cream needs different claims, packaging, and price band for Southeast Asia versus Europe.
Which brands have you worked with?
30+ brands served across China and overseas, covering beauty & personal care, oral care, mother & baby, and other categories. Also licensed products under international IPs such as Disney, How to Train Your Dragon, and The Smurfs.
Do you handle overseas compliance and logistics?
Yes. With a local base in Indonesia, we arrange certifications, compliance clearance, and international shipping end to end. Southeast Asia orders can also be fulfilled locally from the Indonesian plant, saving on tariffs and transit time.
